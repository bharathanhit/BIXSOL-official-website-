## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **A/B Testing**: Run 3-4 different ad variations simultaneously
2. **Frequency Cap**: Set to 3-5 impressions per user per week to avoid ad fatigue
3. **Placement**: Use "Automatic Placements" initially, then optimize to feed placements
4. **Time Targeting**: Focus on business hours (9 AM - 6 PM weekdays)
5. **Retargeting**: Create a list of website visitors and retarget them
6. **Lookalike Audiences**: Create 1-5% lookalike audiences from your page visitors
